FEATURES

Everything Parents Need, In One Place

Built around the real everyday healthcare journey of Cambodian mothers and fathers.

01

National Immunization Schedule

Pre-loaded with Cambodia's complete NIP protocol aligned with the Ministry of Health.

02

Smart Reminders & Due Dates

Automated countdowns calculated from your baby's birth date so booster shots are never forgotten.

03

WHO Growth Monitoring

Track height & weight plotted against official World Health Organization percentiles (P3 to P97).

Helping Cambodian parents care with confidence, stay informed, and celebrate every milestone.
