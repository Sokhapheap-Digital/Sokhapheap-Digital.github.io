ABOUT KOMA

Making child health
easier to manage.

We created KOMA because keeping track of a child's health can become difficult when important records, dates, and milestones are spread across different places.

Our goal is simple: give parents one friendly place to stay organized, remember important vaccinations, and follow their child's journey as they grow.
