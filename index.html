About us

The Problem We Solve

Many parents face challenges in managing their children's healthcare needs. They may forget important vaccination dates, lose paper-based health records, or lack reliable information about child health, nutrition, and development. When children miss important vaccinations, they may not receive the protection they need against preventable diseases, which can put their health at risk. These challenges make it harder for parents to ensure their children receive proper care on time. KOMA addresses these problems by providing a simple digital platform for vaccination tracking, timely reminders, and child healthcare information.

Our Solution

KOMA provides a range of digital tools to help parents take better care of their children's health. Parents can keep vaccination records organized, track upcoming vaccines, and receive smart reminders so they don't miss important vaccination dates. KOMA also offers growth tracking, age-based nutrition recommendations, and useful child healthcare information to help parents make informed decisions throughout their children's growth journey.

📋 Organized Vaccination Records ⏰ Smart Due Date Reminders 📈 WHO Growth & Nutrition Tracking

Our Mission

Our mission is to make child healthcare easier, more accessible, and more reliable for every Cambodian family through technology. We aim to help parents manage their children's health records, stay on track with vaccination schedules, and make informed healthcare decisions. We also aim to reduce the number of children who miss important vaccinations by providing timely reminders and easy access to reliable health information, helping protect children from vaccine-preventable diseases.

Our Vision

We envision a Cambodia where every parent has access to reliable health information and every child has the opportunity to grow up healthy. We hope to contribute to a more connected and digitally supported child healthcare system across the country.

Who We Serve

KOMA mainly serves children aged 0–2 years old and their parents or caregivers. Young children are our main beneficiaries because they need important vaccinations and proper healthcare during their early years. KOMA helps parents and caregivers keep track of their children's vaccination schedules, receive timely reminders, and manage health records in one place. It also provides helpful information about children's growth, nutrition, and development, making it easier for parents to understand and take care of their children's health.

Meet Our Team

We are a team of passionate students who believe technology can make a positive difference in people's lives. With a shared interest in innovation and child healthcare, we created KOMA to address real challenges faced by Cambodian families. Our goal is to continue improving our platform and making child healthcare easier and more accessible through technology.

KOMA Features

KOMA offers simple tools to help parents take care of their children's health. From vaccination reminders to growth tracking and food tips, KOMA helps parents stay informed at every stage of their child's growth.

Vaccination & Schedule

1. Vaccination Tracking & Reminders

Keep your child's vaccination records organized and never miss an important vaccination date.

  • View vaccination schedules based on your child's age.
  • Track completed, upcoming, and due vaccines.
  • Get reminders for upcoming vaccinations.
  • Keep all vaccination records in one place.
Growth & Development

2. Growth Tracking

Follow your child's growth and see how they develop over time.

  • Record your child's height and weight.
  • Check growth progress over time.
  • Keep track of important growth information.
Nutrition & Food Guide

3. Nutrition Guidance

Learn more about your child's food needs at different ages.

  • Explore food tips based on your child's age.
  • Learn about suitable foods for each growth stage.
  • Get helpful information about healthy eating.
